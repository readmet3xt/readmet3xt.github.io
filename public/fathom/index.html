Fathom

A cash-flow copilot for people whose income arrives in lumps. It replaces the balance with one forward-looking number — what is safe to spend today — and shows how sure it is.

Six screens, fully linked. Everything you can tap is real.

Worth trying

  1. From the home screen, tap “Why this number” — every figure opens into its own derivation, including what was deliberately left out.
  2. Tap the ribbon to zoom from seventeen days to six weeks, and watch the conservative floor break through zero on 15 Sep.
  3. Tap the 15 Sep warning for three fixes, each priced.
  4. Open Check in the tab bar for the counter moment: RM 189, answered as a cost rather than a yes or no.
